GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

Let's clear
things up.

No jargon. Just straightforward answers to common questions.

Is Alex really an AI assistant?+

Yes. Alex is an example of a conversational AI voice assistant. The voice and conversation style can be tailored for different business needs.

Can I choose the voice and what it says?+

Yes. The voice, tone and approved business information can be configured. We would work with you to test the responses and make sure they fit your customers.

Could it answer calls when I'm busy?+

That is one of the main use cases. Coverage arrangements, call routing and supported tasks depend on your phone setup and the services connected during implementation.

Can it take bookings automatically?+

Potentially. Booking requires a compatible calendar or booking system and a properly tested integration. We will discuss your requirements before promising that functionality.

What if my business only gets a few calls?+

A few enquiries can still be important, but a voice assistant isn't automatically the right choice for everyone. A short conversation helps us work out whether it would add real value.

Is AI Digital Prime a UK business?+

AI Digital Prime operates as a brand of CALINESCU TECHNOLOGIES LTD. Project scope, support and service arrangements are discussed directly before any agreement.
