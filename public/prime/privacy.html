Website enquiries

In this preview the form either prepares an email draft or copies it locally. Once secure callback delivery is configured, the details you voluntarily provide (name, email, optional business name, phone number where a callback is requested, and message) are sent to the website Worker and a configured email delivery provider for the purpose of responding to your enquiry. No call or meeting is automatically booked. The operator must publish verified contact details, legal basis and retention before enabling delivery. Do not enter sensitive personal information.

Prime Assistant website chat

The website includes a local guided chat preview limited to 10 questions daily per browser. If live AI chat is enabled, conversation messages are transmitted through the website Worker to a configured AI provider, with additional server-enforced usage limits. Before enabling it, publish verified details about the provider, purpose, lawful basis, storage, retention and your privacy contact, and explain the processing to visitors. Avoid entering sensitive personal data.

Voice demonstrations

The website lets you enter public business name, type, location, opening hours, services and prices to personalise a two-minute AI receptionist demonstration. The spoken demo is disabled until a secured API connection is configured. When enabled, these business details and live microphone audio will be processed by ElevenLabs for the purpose of generating a realistic demonstration. The dedicated demo agent is configured without audio recording and with one-day conversation retention, subject to the provider’s operational policies. A full public privacy notice, provider disclosure, lawful basis and consent mechanism must be reviewed before launch.
